About

I design and ship machine learning systems — from synthetic-data pipelines that eliminate labelling bottlenecks to serverless inference endpoints that monitor and retrain themselves. The model converging is the easy part; I care about what comes after it.

Right now I’m an ML / CV research intern at Itaipu Parquetec, building 50K-image synthetic datasets and Detectron2 + SAM2 segmentation pipelines for drone-based transmission-line inspection. My side projects run in production too: a bird-call classifier on AWS Lambda with a consent-gated, human-reviewed retraining loop, and a multi-task CNN for XRD phase identification trained entirely on synthetic spectra.

I’m comfortable across the whole data stack, too — from Excel (MOS-certified) to PyTorch training pipelines to Lambda inference. When I’m not shipping, I’m usually reading post-mortems and tightening someone’s CI pipeline.
